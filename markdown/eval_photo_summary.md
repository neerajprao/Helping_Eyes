# Photo evaluation: summary

148 pharmacy photos (blister strips, cartons, bottles, tubes and personal-care items), taken with a phone, run on 2026-10-23 at commit `5143bf2` (with uncommitted changes). Generated by `eval/run_photo_eval.py score report`; do not edit by hand.

**Reference:** Claude (the assistant model) read every photo itself, before seeing any engine's text, and wrote down the key printed lines and fields. It is one careful reader, not a typed ground truth checked by a second person: where Claude misread a pack, an engine is marked wrong for it. Fields Claude could not read are left out of the scores. Numbers in brackets are 95 % bootstrap intervals over photos.

- Problems the app's quality check flagged (on the text area): none.
- Photos with glare blobs drawn: 15; with blurry text patches: 13; with dark patches: 126; with a measured slant: 64.
- Item outline found: 134 of 148 (none where the item is not one solid four-sided shape, such as a shelf of several products or a crumpled strip).
- Version the capture path kept: flattened 14, original 134.
- Glare check against Claude's lighting label: both say glare 0, only the app 0, only Claude 44, neither 104.

**What the image checks show, and their limits** (seen on the annotated photos):

- The quality check flagged no photo: it calls glare only for compact *saturated* white blobs covering at least 1 % of the picture. Most glare on these packs is a reflection on foil or gloss that is bright but not saturated (or, on mirror-like cartons, a dark reflected image), so it is not caught. Magenta boxes show the saturated blobs that do exist.
- Blue "too dark" patches are 40 px tiles darker than the app's dark threshold (mean below 50). On these photos they are mostly dark-coloured printing (navy labels, black bands) and shadows at the edges, not poor light: the whole-picture brightness never fell below the threshold.
- The item outline (yellow) is grown from the item's own text with GrabCut (`find_item_quad()` in `vision.py`, corrected on 23 October 2026). The earlier outline took the brightest region as the page; on a light floor that is the floor, so 144 of its 145 outlines were the picture's frame and none enclosed a pack, and "how much the item fills" counted the floor too (median 69 % instead of about 22 %).
- Red blurry patches are tiles on recognised text lines whose sharpness (variance of the Laplacian) is below the app's threshold of 60.

**Scoring check:** two random samples of 40 scored answers were read by hand against the photos. The first found three rule mistakes (a date written "December 31, 2026", "1x10 tablets" against "10 tablets", O against 0 in batch codes), which were fixed before these numbers; the second found 1 of 40 wrong, caused by a prescription flag in the reference that the photo did not show (the prescription question is now scored only where Rx, Schedule H or a prescription line is visible).
